About LivingDex.app

LivingDex.app is a fast, privacy-first, client-side tracker for building and sharing your living Pokédex progress across supported Pokémon games.

📘 How LivingDex Works

📦 30-Slot Box Alignment
LivingDex automatically arranges Pokémon into standard 30-slot PC boxes matching in-game storage.
📑 Modular Forms & Expansions
Use Segments to toggle optional content (DLC, Unown, Vivillon, Alcremie, Regional forms) and reorder sections to fit your personal collection.
⚡ Batch Progress Tracking
On PC, hold Shift and click to mark a range. On mobile or touch devices, press & hold a Pokémon slot and drag across slots to batch mark them caught or uncaught.
✨ Shiny & Share Mode
Switch between Normal and Shiny trackers with ✨, and share your exact progress snapshot using the 🔗 Share button.

Features

  • 🔒 Privacy First: Progress stays in your browser (no account required).
  • 📱 PWA Ready: Works offline and can be installed as an app.
  • ⚡ Lightweight: Fast load times with instant search and filtering.
