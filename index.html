About LivingDex.app

LivingDex.app is a static, privacy-first tracker for building and sharing your living Pokédex progress across supported Pokémon games.

Project page: github.com/PimBARF/LivingDex

Credits: Built and maintained by PimBARF, with species and form data provided by PokeAPI.
