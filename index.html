About LivingDex.app

LivingDex.app is a fast, privacy-first, client-side tracker for building and sharing your living Pokédex progress across supported Pokémon games.

Features

  • 🔒 Privacy First: Progress stays in your browser (no account required).
  • 📱 PWA Ready: Works offline and can be installed as an app.
  • ⚡ Lightweight: Fast load times with instant search and filtering.
